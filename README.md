## Dungeon Crawler Carl

*Dungeon Crawler Carl* is a comedic LitRPG series by Matt Dinniman. When an alien intergalactic game show turns Earth into a deadly, multi-level dungeon, ex-coal miner Carl and his ex-girlfriend's cat, Princess Donut, have to survive. Each floor is full of monsters, traps, and absurd rules. Viewers across the galaxy watch and bet on the contestants, and Carl and Donut collect loot, levels, and achievements as they go. The series is known for its dark humor, game-mechanics detail, and sharp satire of reality TV and corporate bureaucracy.

## Layout

- `CLAUDE.md`: instructions for the regulation-watch routine.
- `sources.md`: the allowed official sources.
- `context/`: product and control cards the routine reads before writing; start at `context/index.md`.
- `.claude/skills/`: one skill per step of the routine.
