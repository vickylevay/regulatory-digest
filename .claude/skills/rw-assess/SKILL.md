# Apply to features and rate impact

Inputs: kept items, product facts in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Apply to features

For each item decide which of AI Assistant, AI Variance Analysis and Detect it touches, and why, using only the product facts and the item's own scope text. If the scope text was not read (only a landing page or Q&A read, or a PDF only grepped), state exactly what was and was not read. Phrase conclusions as "my reading". Where scope depends on a legal question, recommend counsel.

For each item also capture: what it requires, key dates (as stated on the page), and the first thing FloQast should check.

## Rate impact

- **High**: currently applicable or enforceable, AND it plausibly attaches to a named feature on the product facts as stated. Action needed now.
- **Medium**: applicable soon (within about 6 months of today) or scope to FloQast unresolved after reading the official text, with a decision or deadline ahead.
- **Low**: voluntary guidance, finalised rules whose trigger (decisions about individuals, Annex III use cases) the features do not meet, delays that reduce obligations, or no in-window change.

State the reason in one clause. If the official text was not read well enough to rate with confidence, rate on what was read and say so.

Never invent article numbers, fines or dates.
